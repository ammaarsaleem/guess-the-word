Getting it onto Facebook
  • Recording happens live, so a 40-second video takes 40 seconds. Leave the tab in the foreground while it runs.
  • Chrome and Edge produce the cleanest files. If your browser saves a .webm, Facebook still accepts it — but converting to MP4 first gives more reliable playback on older phones.
  • Puzzles read best when each picture is unmistakable on a small screen. Swap any emoji that needs a second look, or upload your own photo for a part.
  • Ask the question in your post text too. Comments are what push the video into other people's feeds.
